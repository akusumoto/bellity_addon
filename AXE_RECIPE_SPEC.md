# Chotto Netherite Axe recipe

The Chotto Netherite Axe (`bellity:noboru_netherite_axe`) is craftable at a crafting table with this shaped 3×3 pattern. `-` means an empty slot.

```text
N N -
- B -
- B -
```

- `N`: `minecraft:netherite_ingot`
- `B`: `minecraft:stick`
- Output: one `bellity:noboru_netherite_axe`
- Recipe format: `1.20.10`, with `AlwaysUnlocked` unlock context, matching the other recipes.

Validation must check the exact pattern, ingredient IDs, result, and unlock data. Rebuild the `.mcaddon` and update the installed development packs for an in-game crafting check.
