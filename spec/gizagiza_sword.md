## Special Ability

Knockback upon hitting an entity is doubled compared to using a normal sword under the same conditions. Basically, it applies an additional knockback of the same degree to the target, on top of the knockback generated by a normal attack.

The special ability is implemented using the custom item component `bellity:double_knockback_on_hit` and `behavior_pack/scripts/main.js`. It applies an additional horizontal knockback of strength 0.4 from the attacker to the target, with no additional vertical knockback. If their horizontal positions match, use the attacker's line of sight; if the horizontal direction still cannot be determined, do not perform additional processing. Check consistency with standard Minecraft behavior on an actual device for differences caused by knockback resistance, sprint attacks, knockback enchantments, etc.
